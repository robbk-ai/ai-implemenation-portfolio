# Construction Automation
This section demonstrates real-world examples of how AI can automate, enhance, or streamline construction project management workflows.

## 🏗️ Use Case: Daily Log Automation
**Problem:** Manual daily logs take 30–90 minutes and are often inconsistent.  
**AI Solution:** Use AI to generate structured daily logs from:
- Meeting notes  
- Field observations  
- Mobile voice memos  
- Photo descriptions  

**Outcome:** Saves 5–10 hours per week.

---

## 🧱 Use Case: Change Order Summarization
**Problem:** Change orders require reading specs, emails, and drawings.  
**AI Solution:** AI summarizes:
- Scope changes  
- Cost impacts  
- Schedule conflicts  
- Responsible trade partners  

**Outcome:** Faster owner communication and fewer errors.

---

## 📑 Use Case: Submittal Review Assistance
**Problem:** Submittal reviews get bottlenecked due to missing or unclear information.  
**AI Solution:** AI flags:
- Missing documents  
- Spec non-compliance  
- Conflicts with other trades  
- Required follow-up questions  

**Outcome:** Improves submittal quality and saves PM review time.
