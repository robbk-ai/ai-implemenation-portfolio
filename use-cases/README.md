# Use Cases

This folder contains short, practical examples of how AI can solve real business problems.  
Each use case includes:  
- The business problem  
- The AI-powered solution  
- Workflow impact  
- Example prompts  
- Potential ROI

---

## 📌 1. AI-Powered Meeting Summaries  
A GenAI assistant that transforms long meetings into actionable summaries.

**Business Problem:**  
Teams waste time manually pulling action items, risks, and decisions from meetings.

**AI Solution:**  
Use an LLM to generate:  
- Action items  
- Accountability assignments  
- Decisions made  
- Risks and blockers  
- Next steps  

**Workflow Impact:**  
Saves ~30–60 minutes per meeting.

**Example Prompt:**  
Summarize the meeting transcript into:

Key decisions

Assigned action items

Risks

Next steps
Use bullet points and assign owners.

## 📌 2. Document → Insight Extraction  
AI that extracts structured insights from long documents (contracts, specs, manuals, proposals).

**Business Problem:**  
Employees spend hours locating requirements or important details inside large documents.

**AI Solution:**  
Model extracts:  
- Key requirements  
- Dates, numbers, specs  
- Risks and obligations  
- Change impacts  

**Workflow Impact:**  
Cuts reading time by 70%+.

**Example Prompt:**  Extract all key requirements, important numbers, deadlines, and risks
from the following text. Present them as a structured list.

## 📌 3. Customer Email Intent Classification  
AI that sorts customer messages by urgency and category.

**Business Problem:**  
Teams manually identify priority tickets, emails, and customer needs.

**AI Solution:**  
AI auto-classifies into:  
- High priority  
- Medium priority  
- Low priority  
- Billing  
- Support  
- Sales  
- Escalations  

**Workflow Impact:**  
Improves response speed and reduces human triage effort.

**Example Prompt:**  
Classify the following customer email into:

Intent

Urgency

Recommended action
Then generate a suggested reply.

## 📌 4. AI Risk Detection for Projects  
AI analyzes project updates to surface risks before they become problems.

**Business Problem:**  
Risk management is reactive instead of proactive.

**AI Solution:**  
Use GenAI to highlight:  
- Schedule delays  
- Budget risks  
- Team capacity issues  
- Vendor/subcontractor risks  
- Blockers  

**Workflow Impact:**  
Helps teams intervene early → prevents costly overruns.

**Example Prompt:**  
Identify all project risks in the following weekly update.
Rate each risk as High, Medium, or Low and explain why.

## 📌 5. SOP Generation From Minimal Input  
AI converts rough notes or fragmented instructions into polished SOPs.

**Business Problem:**  
Teams skip documentation because it’s slow and tedious.

**AI Solution:**  
AI turns:  
- Bullets  
- Chat logs  
- Videos  
- Screenshots  
Into clean, step-by-step SOPs.

**Workflow Impact:**  
Standardizes processes and improves training speed.

**Example Prompt:**  Turn the following notes into a clear, step-by-step SOP
with roles, tools needed, and quality checks.
