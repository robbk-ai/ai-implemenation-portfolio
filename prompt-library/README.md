# Prompt Library – Practical Business & AI Prompts

This prompt library provides reusable, business-ready prompts for AI tools such as  
ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, or any LLM. Each prompt is written for clarity, repeatability,  
and real-world implementation. These are the exact patterns used across automation,  
analysis, workflow optimization, and communication support.

---

## 1️⃣ Email Triage & Communication

**Triage Prompt**

> You are my email triage assistant. For each email, classify it with:  
> - Category: Urgent / Action Needed / Info Only / Low Priority  
> - Owner: Me / Someone else (specify)  
> - Due: Today / This Week / No Deadline / Ignore  
> - Summary: 1–2 sentences  
> - Suggested next action  

**Thread Summary Prompt**

> Summarize this email thread into 5 bullet points. Highlight:  
> - Main decision(s)  
> - Open questions  
> - What I am responsible for  
> - Any due dates or risks  

---

## 2️⃣ Decision-Making & Risk Analysis

**Decision Support Prompt**

> Act as my decision analysis assistant. Evaluate the following decision with:  
> - Options  
> - Pros & cons of each  
> - Risks  
> - Required info  
> - Your recommendation  
> Here is the situation: [PASTE].

**Risk Identification Prompt**

> Identify the risks in this plan. Classify them as:  
> - High / Medium / Low  
> - Operational / Financial / Client / Schedule  
> Provide a mitigation strategy for each.

## 3️⃣ SOP & Documentation Creation

**SOP Builder Prompt**

> Write a step-by-step Standard Operating Procedure for the following task.  
> Include:  
> - Purpose  
> - Required tools  
> - Step-by-step instructions  
> - Quality checks  
> - Common failure points  
> Task: [PASTE].

**Process Explanation Prompt**

> Explain this process in clear, simple language for a non-technical user.  
> Include visuals or bullet lists if helpful.  
> Process description: [PASTE].

---

## 4️⃣ Workflow Automation Prompts

**Workflow Mapping Prompt**

> Create a structured workflow map for this task. Include:  
> - Inputs  
> - Outputs  
> - Responsible roles  
> - Steps (1–10)  
> - Automation opportunities  
> Task description: [PASTE].

**Automation Suggestion Prompt**

> Evaluate this workflow and list the tasks that could be automated using AI.  
> For each task, include:  
> - Tool(s) recommended  
> - Expected time saved  
> - Risks or limitations  
> Workflow: [PASTE].

## 5️⃣ Communication & Writing Support

**Professional Email Drafting Prompt**

> Draft a professional email reply in my voice. Keep it concise and direct.  
> Here is the email I am responding to: [PASTE].  
> Here are the points I want to make: [LIST].

**Rewrite & Improve Prompt**

> Rewrite this text to be clearer, more concise, and professional.  
> Maintain the original meaning. Text: [PASTE].

**Extract Key Info Prompt**

> - Decisions
